EPA (expected points added) measures how much each play changed the offense's expected points, from cfbfastR's model. A QB's plays are his dropbacks (passes, sacks, scrambles) plus designed runs.

Garbage time removed: plays when the margin is over 38 in Q2, over 28 in Q3 or over 22 in Q4. Qualifier: Power 4 is the SEC, Big Ten, Big 12, ACC and Notre Dame.
